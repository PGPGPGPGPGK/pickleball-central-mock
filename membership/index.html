FREQUENTLY ASKED

What is the difference between the four plans?

Mainly the commitment length and the price — every plan includes the same 30 free playing hours a month, and paying for a longer stretch up front costs less per month, from ₹2,500 monthly down to ₹1,500 a month on the yearly plan. The longer terms also carry a bigger discount on additional booking hours and F&B, and more guest passes.

Is there a lock-in period?

The monthly plan is month-to-month with no lock-in — cancel any time before your next billing date. The quarterly, half-yearly and yearly plans are prepaid for their full term, which is what earns the lower rate.

Can I switch to a longer plan later?

Yes. Talk to us at reception or on WhatsApp and we will move you across — the unused portion of your current term is adjusted against the new one.

What happens if my membership lapses?

You get a 3-day grace period. After that, you revert to standard walk-in rates. Rejoin anytime to resume your member benefits.

Are the prices inclusive of GST?

Yes. Every price shown includes 18% GST — the amount on the card is the amount you pay.
