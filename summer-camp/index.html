Summer camp FAQs

Who is the summer camp for?

All ages and all levels. We run separate batches for beginners, intermediate, and advanced players, so first-timers and regular players each get the right coaching.

Do I need my own paddle?

No — paddles and balls are provided for every session. Just bring sports shoes, comfortable clothes, and a water bottle.

How much does the camp cost?

Early-bird pricing starts at ₹2,499. Seats are limited per batch, so the early-bird rate is best confirmed in advance on WhatsApp.

Where is the camp held?

At Pickleball Central in Peerzadiguda, Hyderabad — minutes from Uppal, Habsiguda, Nacharam, Boduppal, and Ghatkesar. On-site parking is available.
