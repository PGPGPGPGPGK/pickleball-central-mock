Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Pickleball Central, a unit of Snowflake Enterprises (“Pickleball Central”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, discloses, and safeguards your information when you use our website at pickleball-central.co.in, our mobile application, and the related booking, membership, coaching, and event services (together, the “Services”).

We are committed to handling your personal data responsibly and in accordance with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”) and other applicable laws. By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

1. Who we are (Data Fiduciary)

Pickleball Central is operated by Snowflake Enterprises, a sports facility based in Peerzadiguda, Hyderabad, Telangana, India (GSTIN 36AFNFS2231R1ZC). For the purposes of the DPDP Act, we act as the Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data. You can reach us using the contact details in Section 13.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & identity data — your name, mobile phone number (used as your primary login via one-time password), email address, and, where you choose to provide it, your profile photo and skill level.
  • Booking & activity data — court bookings, memberships, coaching sessions, tournament and event registrations, attendance, match and play history, and your in-app preferences.
  • Payment data — payments are processed by our payment partner, Razorpay. We do not store your full card, UPI, or bank credentials on our servers. We retain transaction references, amounts, payment status, and GST invoice details needed to fulfil your order and meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • Device & technical data — device type and identifiers, operating system, app version, IP address, push notification tokens, and diagnostic or log data generated when you use the Services.
  • Usage & analytics data — pages and screens viewed, features used, referring URLs, and similar interaction data collected through cookies and analytics tools (see Section 7).
  • Communications — records of messages you exchange with us, including support queries, WhatsApp messages, email, and in-app notifications.

We do not knowingly collect precise GPS location. We do not collect sensitive personal data unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, a medical note relevant to a coaching or camp enrolment).

3. How we use your information

We use your personal data to:

  • create and manage your account and authenticate you via OTP;
  • process court bookings, memberships, coaching, tournaments, events, and food-and-beverage orders;
  • process payments, issue GST-compliant invoices, and handle refunds and cancellations;
  • send you transactional messages — booking confirmations, reminders, payment receipts, waitlist updates, and account notices — by SMS, email, WhatsApp, and push notification;
  • provide customer support and respond to your requests, feedback, and complaints;
  • operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Services, including preventing fraud, abuse, and misuse;
  • send you promotional or marketing communications where you have consented, and from which you can opt out at any time;
  • comply with applicable legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.

4. Legal basis for processing

Under the DPDP Act, we process your personal data on the basis of the consent you provide when you create an account and use the Services, and for the “legitimate uses” permitted by law — for example, where you have voluntarily provided data for a specified purpose, or where processing is necessary to fulfil a service you requested or to comply with a legal obligation. You may withdraw your consent at any time as described in Section 9; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before the withdrawal.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to run the Services, with the following categories of recipients (each acting as our Data Processor under contract):

  • Razorpay — payment processing, subscriptions, and refunds.
  • MSG91 — sending and verifying SMS one-time passwords for login.
  • Resend — sending transactional and, where applicable, marketing email.
  • Interakt — sending WhatsApp notifications and support messages.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) — delivering push notifications to your device.
  • Google Analytics / Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, and Meta — website and app analytics and measurement (see Section 7).
  • Cloudflare and our hosting provider — content delivery, security, and infrastructure.

We may also disclose information where required by law, court order, or a government authority, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of Pickleball Central, our users, or the public. If our business is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred subject to this Policy.

6. International transfers

Some of our service providers process data on servers located outside India. Where this happens, we take reasonable steps to ensure your data is protected by appropriate safeguards and is transferred only to jurisdictions and recipients permitted under applicable law.

7. Cookies and analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the Services are used. We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity, and the Meta Pixel to measure traffic and improve our content and campaigns. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling them may affect some features.

8. Data retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, and thereafter for the period required to comply with our legal, tax, and accounting obligations (for example, GST invoice records must be retained for the period prescribed by law), to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements. When data is no longer required, we delete or anonymise it.

9. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • access and obtain a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction or updating of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request erasure of your personal data where it is no longer required;
  • withdraw consent you previously gave;
  • opt out of marketing communications at any time;
  • nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity, as provided under the DPDP Act.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hi@pickleball-central.co.in. We will respond within a reasonable period as required by law. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Deleting your account and data

You can request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time from within the mobile app: go to Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account and confirm. Doing so immediately deactivates your account— you are signed out and can no longer log in — and submits your deletion request to us. If you prefer, you may instead request deletion by emailing hi@pickleball-central.co.in from your registered email address, or by contacting us on +91 97007 44634 from your registered phone number, with the subject “Delete my account”.

Once we action your request, we delete or anonymise your personal data (including your name, email, phone number, profile photos, and community posts) within 30 days, except for records we are legally required to retain — such as payment and GST invoice records, which we keep for the statutory period and then delete. If you change your mind before your data is erased, contact us and we can reactivate your account.

11. Children's privacy

The Services are intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. Where a child participates in our coaching programmes, summer camps, or youth events, their registration must be completed by a parent or legal guardian, who provides and consents to the processing of the child’s information for that purpose. If you believe a child has provided us personal data without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

12. Data security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction — including encryption in transit, access controls, and audit logging. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a personal data breach, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required by law.

If you have questions, concerns, or complaints about this Policy or how we handle your data, please contact our Grievance Officer:

14. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and, where the changes are significant, provide a more prominent notice. Your continued use of the Services after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
